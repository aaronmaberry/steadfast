# Podcast Intro/Outro Music — Research Takeaways (Walk Steadfast)

## What the top shows do
- **The Daily (NYT):** Original theme by Wonderly (piano, viola/strings, drums, electric bass, guitar). Propulsive and urgent. Usually starts with a **cold open** (voice first, no music), then the theme creeps in under the host. Producers re-arrange the theme's stems per episode, so the identity stays the same while the mood changes. This is the model for building one sonic identity across intro, outro and stingers.
- **Huberman Lab:** A short recurring music bed under a scripted welcome ("Welcome to the Huberman Lab podcast, where we discuss..."). The music sits low and the voice carries the hook. The opening runs long (sponsors), which is a weakness.
- **Diary of a CEO:** About 60-90 s of rapid cold-open clips (a shocking quote, quick questions, a cliff-hanger cut), then a short theme sting. The hook is the content, and the music is the punctuation.
- **SmartLess:** A theme plays under host banter, and the "Smart...Less" vocal tag returns as a recurring audio logo. Loose, but instantly recognizable.
- **Joe Rogan Experience:** A short, consistent jingle/bumper and then straight into the conversation. It skips teasers, so it relies on brand recognition.
- **BibleProject / Art of Manliness:** Faith and men's shows use a calm, warm, short instrumental identity (acoustic/cinematic) under a one-line welcome. Tasteful, not hype.

## Industry best practices
- **Branded music sting: 3-10 s.** The whole open stays under 30 s, matching Aaron's rule that the first 30 seconds hook the listener.
- **Cold open (15-40 s of the best moment), then the sting, then a one-line welcome** consistently beats music-first openings.
- **Stinger vs bed:** a *stinger* is a short hit that punctuates (intro and segment transitions). A *bed* is a sparse loopable track that sits under speech (welcome VO, outro CTA). Use the same instrumentation in different arrangements.
- **Voice over music:** the music fades or ducks as the voice enters. Keep the bed about 12-20 dB under speech (bed around -18 to -20 LUFS against a voice at -14). Use fades, never hard cuts. Check the mix on phone speakers.
- **No vocals/lyrics** in beds, because they compete with speech.
- **Outro:** 15-30 s. It's a slower or softer version of the intro motif with room for the CTA ("subscribe, walksteadfast.com") and ends on a resolved chord.
- **Never change it.** One signature, kept for years, builds recognition.

## Recommended Walk Steadfast format
1. Cold open: 15-25 s of the strongest Wes/Jonathon moment.
2. Intro sting: about 8 s (these files). Voice enters on the tail: "This is Walk Steadfast..."
3. Outro bed: about 25 s under the closing CTA. Duck it under the voice, then let the final chord ring out.
4. Optional: cut a 2-3 s stinger from the intro's resolving hit for segment transitions.
