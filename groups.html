Tables · crews · churches

Same 24 weeks. Shared obedience.

A crew of four or a Saturday men’s ministry. You get the lectures, the workbook, and the leader scripts. The app keeps the week in each man’s pocket. There is no leaderboard. There is a named act and a brother who knows if it got done.

How a room runs the 24 weeks

Progress without a scoreboard.

01 · Size

Three to seven men

Small enough that a man cannot hide. A church can run many tables. Do not put forty men in one circle and call it accountability.

02 · Covenant

Write the rules once

We show up. We tell the truth. We do not fix each other in the parking lot. We do not repeat what was said. Men respect a contract more than a vibe.

03 · Pair

One brother per mark

Each man names one brother for the six weeks. Midweek he asks: did you do the act? The app can ping the pair. Shame stays off the stage.

04 · The week

Watch. Write. One named act.

Lecture, workbook, then one thing that happens in the house. Next meeting starts with that act, not with feelings. Miss it, say so, pick it up. No points.

05 · The table score

How many of us did the act

The room tracks one number: 5 of 7. The man who missed is not on a public board. The table either carried the week or it did not.

06 · Close the mark

A meal, not a badge

Weeks 6, 12, 18, 24: eat together, pray where they can hear it, speak one sentence to wife or son. The app only records done. The ceremony is at the table.
