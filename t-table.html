How the hour runs

Watch. Write. One act.

Three to seven men. Small enough that a man cannot hide. You do not need to become a curriculum expert. You name the Tuesday, you put a passage under it, you send them home with one thing they can fail at.

Next week starts with that act, not with feelings. Miss it, say so, pick it up. No points. No public board. The table either carried the week or it did not.

Credit where it is due: the four marks — reject passivity, accept responsibility, lead courageously, expect the greater reward — were popularized by Robert Lewis. The exposition and the 24 weeks here are original.

The taste · chapter 1

A Quiet Tuesday

A man’s life is decided on Tuesdays. Not the Sunday he stands up in church. The day in the middle, with nothing special in it. The day he comes home tired and the house is loud and there is a conversation he could have or not have.

Anthony left for work at 6:10. He let his father’s call go to voicemail. He stood in the doorway between the garage and the kitchen and was not sure why he was not walking in. Then he kissed his wife on the side of her head without looking at her. That is not a kiss. That is a fly-by.

Nothing in that day would make a movie. The mark was passivity, and it looked like a Tuesday.
