**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
What does it look like to honor a father who is still alive when you do not want to pretend?

Jonathon's dad has called about twice a year for twenty-eight years. Every call ends the same way: "Well. I'll let you go." Jonathon has never once dialed first. A five-word email from his boss showed him the gap. Wes reports on a Saturday shoe shine with his oldest, and on the pot roast where he and his brother finally asked their mother what she wants. She took eleven seconds. Her answer was not either brother's plan.

Exodus 20:12 says to honor your father and your mother. Honor is weight, not a halo and not nothing. If your dad is safe, go first. Ask one question about his life before you. Then stay in the quiet.

This week's challenge: call him, ask one question, and let him talk.

## This week's challenge

1. If your dad is alive and safe, **call him**. Do not wait for a birthday.
2. Ask **one question** about his life before you.
3. When it gets quiet, **do not fill it with the weather**.
4. **Married:** tell your wife before you call.
5. **If he is gone:** ask someone who knew him.
6. **If he is dangerous:** do not call. Write the question. Pray his name. Talk to a counselor.
