## SEGMENT 2 · Glad you're mine

WES: Mine was the truck, Easter afternoon. My youngest is sixteen. He said, "Am I almost done with the Saturdays?" I said four more. Then I said, "I want to tell you something that's got nothing to do with the truck. I'm glad you're mine. That's all. Not because of anything." He said, "Okay." A long pause. "Can I drive home?" I let him. I held the handle above the door the whole way. I did not add a because. I have added because to every kind word for fifty years. I wanted one without a score.

JONATHON: That's the well done that is not a prize. I need that next to a like, because I will turn a like into a score. Did he like it enough. Did I pick right. Your sentence has no points. I tried it at bath. I told Theo, "I'm glad you're mine." He said, "I know." Four years old and he already knew. I almost added because you are funny, because you throw over the fence, because. I stopped. I want to be the kind of man who can say it without a receipt.

WES: My dad said he was proud of me when I was forty-three, in a hospital bed. I am not retelling that whole night. I am saying I do not want my boy to wait until a handle is the only thing I can hold. I said it in a truck that smelled like grass. That's enough setting. I almost ruined it by asking if he heard me. He said okay. Okay is enough. If I had asked again I would have been collecting. I have collected enough. I held the handle and I let him drive and I did not add a lecture about the Saturdays. Four more. He knows. He does not need a speech on Easter afternoon. He needed a sentence without a score. I finally had one. I want another one for my wife tonight, and I want to leave it alone after I say it. Leaving it alone is the stay. Talking it to death is the old me.

JONATHON: Theo at church, after the reading about the tomb, said, "Because he came back?" I said yes. He nodded like that settled the universe. I want a faith that nods. ## SEGMENT 3 · The Word

WES: We're about halfway, and I want the Bible open, because a voice from heaven already said the sentence men wait decades to hear. Matthew three, verse seventeen.

JONATHON: I have wanted that voice from a man who posts fish. I will not get it that way. I might get a like. I might get a call in April. I might get nothing. The verse is not about my dad.

WES: "This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased." Before Jesus had preached a sermon. Before a cross. Before a resume. Loved. Pleased. A Father who stays, and who speaks, and who does not wait for a trophy. If you only work for a hospital bed or a tie or a like, you will be hungry on a Monday. Work for this voice. Then say a smaller version in a truck.

JONATHON: I have worked for a tie. I know the hunger on a Monday. I still write emails like I am trying to hear not bad, kid.

WES: I have worked for a hospital bed most of my life without admitting it. I waited until a man could not sit up to hear four words, and then I talked about those four words as if talking were the same as living them, and I still catch myself earning them again in a kitchen. Earning is a hole. This verse fills it if I will let it. I do not let it most Tuesdays. I let a title, or a porch, or a brother call, tell me if I am pleased enough. That is a thin god. I am tired of thin gods. I want this voice in the truck more than I want a handle to hold.

[BOARD: Romans 5:8 (NIV) · "But God demonstrates his own love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us."]

JONATHON: Well pleased before the work. I have built a life on pleased after the work. Cancun. Leaderboard. A kneel in the dirt I hoped the bleachers saw. This verse ruins my pay system. Good. Ruin it.

WES: Ruin it. Then go say glad you're mine to someone who already knows and still needs to hear it. Then, if you owe a girl a sentence about a grandfather in Louisiana, say it this week. Do not wait for a better like. I keep waiting for a better moment the way I waited for a better title. The moment is a Tuesday with leftover ham in the fridge and a boy who already said okay. Take the moment. If you wait for a clean feeling you will get another Easter and another like and another year of far away. I have enough years of far away on my side of the ledger. I do not want more on his.

[BOARD: Luke 15:20 (NIV) · "So he got up and went to his father. But while he was still a long way off, his father saw him and was filled with compassion for him; he ran to his son, threw his arms around him and kissed him."]

## SEGMENT 4 · Pushback

WES: Keep pushing.

JONATHON: A like is not the same as a father who stays. If I sit here glowing about a button, a man whose dad is gone or cruel will think we just sold him a fairy tale. Paint.

WES: Paint. A like is small. God is not small. The chair can still be empty and the tomb can still be empty. Both true. We are not baptizing a profile picture. We are saying you can stay even if he does not. You can speak even if he likes and does not write. You can be well pleased with a boy who has not earned a truck yet.

JONATHON: Second. "I'm glad you're mine" can become a new plaque. I can say it and still miss bath. I can say it and still hand Ruby to Dani.

WES: Then say it and stay. The stay is the pay. The sentence without the stay is a commercial. I said it and I let him drive. That's a stay I can measure in miles and a white knuckle on a handle.

JONATHON: Third. I still have October with Dani. We started. We have not finished. Easter did not finish it. A ham did not finish it. I asked her last night for a night this week. She said Wednesday. I put it on the fridge. If I erase it I will have used Easter as weather again.

WES: Keep Wednesday. I call my older brother tonight at seven. We will tell you Friday. I am not using a like or a tomb as a reason to skip a barn date I already set. I have used holy days as cover before. I left Thanksgiving early and called it traffic. I can leave Easter in a glow and call it peace. It would be a cease-fire again. I am done selling myself cease-fires. Seven o'clock. I will sit in the truck so I do not perform for a kitchen. I will say the house and the will. I will not say I am a better brother now because I remembered a verse. Verses do not make me better. They make me unable to hide with a straight face. I want that more than I want to look like a man who had a nice Easter.
