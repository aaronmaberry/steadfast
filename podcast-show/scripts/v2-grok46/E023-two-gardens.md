## SEGMENT 1 · The last out

WES: Let's take the next few minutes on Saturday, because a garden can look like a dirt infield. You first.

JONATHON: First game. Saturday the twentieth. Lost by one. They tell you they do not keep score. Everybody kept score. One of the twins, the one who is scared of the ball, who asked to bat with her eyes closed, struck out with the bases loaded to end it. I walked out to her first, before any parent could, knelt, and said, "That was a brave swing." She said, "I closed my eyes." I said, "I know. Still brave." I wanted to look at the other dads. I have been looking at other dads my whole life. I went to her instead.

WES: That's go first. Not a speech. Dirt on a knee.

JONATHON: In the parking lot her dad, he works for the power company, said, "Thanks for getting out there first. I froze." Then he said, "No, I wouldn't have. I was looking at the other parents." He said he would be early Thursday. I believed him and I also know how a parking-lot promise feels at supper. We will see Thursday. I want him early. I also want me first again if I have to be.

WES: The first Adam hid. The last Adam walked toward the thing. You walked toward a girl who had just failed in public. That's the picture for this week. Not a win. A walk.

[BOARD: Public dirt is a better pulpit than the fence.]

JONATHON: About three weeks ago in the car Ruby asked, "Daddy, do you have a dad?" I said, "That's a good one to ask Mom, bud." I handed it off. Dani said, "Daddy's dad lives far away, in Louisiana. Daddy will tell you about him." I owe her the answer myself, after Sunday. I can walk out to somebody else's twin and still hide from my own seven-year-old. That's two gardens in one week.

WES: Let me sit with that one. Not this month, you said once, about a nine-year-old thinking his dad does not want him. I heard you. I am not rushing you past Sunday. I am also not letting you keep the handoff as the ending. After Sunday you tell her. We will check. I have handed off hard sentences to my wife for decades and called it teamwork. Sometimes it was. Sometimes it was me putting her between me and a child who asked me first. If a girl asks you, you answer. Mom can fill in. Mom should not be the wall. I did that with a shepherd I skipped and a horn I would not explain. I am late and I am still learning the same walk.

JONATHON: I know I used Dani as a wall. I could feel it in my mouth while I said ask Mom. It was smooth and it was a hide. I told her later in the kitchen I would take the next one. She said, "Good. She asked you." No speech. Just that. I wrote it on a card so I cannot lose it between now and Sunday night. The card says Ruby, then the request, in that order, even though the request feels bigger. The request is a man I barely know. Ruby is a girl in my house. The house goes first. I keep having to write that down like I am slow, because I am slow.

## SEGMENT 2 · The report that could wait

WES: Mine is from spring 2018. My daughter was sixteen. Caught drinking at a party. My wife called me at four. I stayed at work until nine, finishing a report that was not due for a month. She handled all of it. That night she said, "I needed you at four o'clock." I had a garden and I hid in a spreadsheet. I told myself I was being useful. I was being first Adam with a badge.

JONATHON: I'll be real with you, I would have found a report. I would have called it providing. That's the oldest lie I know. What did you do with it this week, because 2018 is a long hide if it is still just a story.

WES: I told my daughter I was sorry I was not there at four. She remembered the night. She did not remember the report. She remembered I was late. I told my wife I was sorry again, which she has heard, and I asked if there is a four o'clock I am still missing. She said, "You ask now. You didn't then." That's a mercy I did not earn. I do not want another report that can wait while a girl sits in a kitchen.

JONATHON: That's go first toward your own house. I needed to hear it next to the dirt, because I will collect the kneel and skip the car. The car is my four o'clock. A question about whether I have a dad, and I handed it to Dani like a report that could wait.

WES: Saturday I was at my older brother's place, one of the twelve Saturdays. I walked out to the barn and said, "We need to talk about Mom's house and her will. Not this week. But I'm done hiding from it." He said, "Not at Easter." I said, "Tuesday after. I'll call." He nodded and went back to his welder. That is not settled. That is a date.

JONATHON: A date is how you stop hiding without blowing up a holy weekend. I keep hoping the other man will start the rest so I do not have to.

WES: I stood there longer than I needed to, hoping the same thing. He did not start. The welder started again. I walked back to the house and I told my wife I had said the sentence. She squeezed my arm and did not make it a meeting. I wanted a meeting. A meeting would have let me stay in the barn in my head all afternoon. I drank coffee instead and I watched him work. That is a smaller walk than a will, and I still had to take it.

JONATHON: I keep thinking a date is the same as done. It is not. I have dated the request all the way to Sunday and I could still flinch at 9:39 and call it prayer. Prayer that always ends in wait is not prayer. It is the trees again. I told Dani that. She said, "Then don't flinch." Two words. Better than my paragraphs. I asked her to ask me at 9:41 if I had done it. She said she would. That's a Thursday text in a marriage. I need it. I am not above needing it. I used to think needing it made me the boy again. It makes me a man who will not hide in a date.

[BOARD: Useful paper can still be a tree to hide behind.]

## SEGMENT 3 · The Word

WES: We're about halfway, and I want the Bible open, because Paul names the two men in one breath. First Corinthians fifteen, verse forty-five.

JONATHON: I have been hiding in trees since Christmas. A friend request from my dad. Profile picture, a man holding a redfish. I look at it about once a week. Fifty-some phone calls in twenty-eight years, and I learned more from that picture than from all of them. I told Dani I will decide Sunday night, Easter, after the kids are down. I am still in the trees until then. I need the verse before I decide.

WES: "The first man Adam became a living being." And then, "the last Adam, a life-giving spirit." First man lives, and hides, and blames, and stays in the trees. Last Adam gives life, and walks toward the thing, and does not wait for the other parents to move. Holy Week is not a mood. It is which man you practice being when a girl strikes out and when a request sits in a phone.

[BOARD: Romans 5:8 (NIV) · "But God demonstrates his own love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us."]

JONATHON: Not after I became the kind of son who knows what to say. Not after I write a perfect message. He walked out first. I have used the trees as wisdom. The trees are fear with leaves on them. I may still say no on Sunday. I may say yes. I will not call the delay wisdom after I have a date.

WES: You have a date. Keep it. Do not move it because the ham is in the oven or because a picture of a fish feels like weather. Weather is how we hid until Thursday. We are done with weather.

[BOARD: Isaiah 53:5 (NIV) · "But he was pierced for our transgressions, he was crushed for our iniquities; the punishment that brought us peace was on him, and by his wounds we are healed."]

JONATHON: He walked the whole way. I have not started mine with Ruby. Two different walks. I do not get to borrow his finished word for my delay.

## SEGMENT 4 · Pushback

WES: Keep pushing.

JONATHON: Some men should not walk toward a father who hurts people. If I sit here talking about a redfish, a man whose dad is dangerous is going to think we just baptized a bad door. Paint, not pencil.

WES: Paint. If a man is dangerous, you do not owe him your kitchen. You owe God a decision, and you owe your kids a safe house. Go first toward wisdom, a pastor, a counselor, not toward a fist. This table is not telling you to open a door that should stay shut. It is telling you to stop calling delay a plan. Jonathon's dad is a request in a phone, not a man in the driveway. Different case. If your case is the driveway, you get a different first step, and you take it with someone who is not us.

JONATHON: Second. Walking out first can become a new performance. I knelt in the dirt and I felt the bleachers anyway. I can collect a kneel.

WES: Then you tell the truth about the bleachers, which you just did. Collecting is first Adam with better posture. Going first and then confessing the hunger to be seen is closer to the last. Do both.

JONATHON: Third. Nine-year-olds don't think, my dad is scared. They think, my dad doesn't want me. You asked me to let you sit with that. I am still sitting. I do not have a pretty answer. I have a Sunday night.

WES: Sit. Then tell Ruby after. She already has far away. She asked if you have one. That's a girl walking out toward you. Meet her.

JONATHON: I know. I keep buying time like time is free. It is not free. She is already writing a story.

WES: I sat with my own girl sixteen years after a horn and I learned that late still counts and that late still costs. Do not buy sixteen years because Sunday feels heavy. Sunday is heavy. A seven-year-old is heavier if you leave her with far away and a promise you do not keep. Keep it. Clumsy. Short. Then stop talking and let her ask the next thing. If she asks whether he likes her, you will want to decorate. Do not decorate. Tell her what is true and what you do not know. Unknown is allowed. A wall is not.

[BOARD: Meet the girl in the car. Do not hand her to Mom.]

## SEGMENT 5 · What we're actually doing

WES: We've got a little under ten minutes. Sunday night you decide. What is the actual motion?

JONATHON: Kids down. Phone. Yes or no on the request. No message either way, not at first. If I write a speech I will be collecting again. I told Dani the hour. She said she will leave the lamp on. I said I do not want her in the room. She said okay. I will tell you Friday what I did. I will also tell Ruby this week, not in a month. That's a second walk, and it is the one I am more afraid of.

WES: I call my brother Tuesday after Easter about the house and the will. I said the date in a barn. I will keep it. I will not hide in a report. I already did that once with a girl who needed me at four.

JONATHON: Thursday I will look for the twin's dad early. If he is late I will still go first to his girl if she needs it. I do not get to make his parking-lot promise my excuse to look at the bleachers again.

WES: And if you are listening and you have a four o'clock, go at four. Not at nine. Not after a report. Four. The last Adam does not hide in work that can wait. I have practiced useful work as a hide for most of my adult life. It photographs well. It still leaves a girl in a kitchen. If your boss would understand a family emergency, this is one, even if nobody is bleeding. Go. Then come back and finish the paper. The paper will keep. The four o'clock will not.

JONATHON: I have used a calendar as a hide too. Sunday night looks holy on a card. If I do not also take the walk in my own hallway, the card was a tree. I told my nephew by text that I have a date and that I am scared of it. He wrote, "same. different door." Twenty-two and he understood. I did not ask him to sit with me. I asked him to pray. He wrote, "ok. thursday still?" Thursday is still. The texts stay. The garden in my house is still mine to walk.

## SEGMENT 6 · This week's challenge

WES: Last few minutes. Pick one hiding place. A phone request. A report. A bleacher. A car question you handed off. Walk toward it this week. One step. Tell one man the step. If the step is a no, make it a clear no with a reason you could say to God, not a delay you could say to a bleacher.

JONATHON: I have two steps. Sunday night, the request. This week, Ruby. I will not use the first as a way to skip the second. I already tried that with Mom in the car. It felt smooth. It was a hide.

WES: I have the Tuesday call and the four o'clock test. If my wife names a four I am missing, I go. I do not finish a paragraph first. I have finished too many paragraphs. I asked her this morning if there is one this week. She said not today, and then she said, "Don't look relieved." I was relieved. I told her so. Relieved is a tell. It means I still want the trees. I will ask again Thursday, and I will try not to look relieved if she names one. That's a small walk inside a marriage, and it is still a walk.

JONATHON: If you live alone, go first toward a brother or toward God in a quiet room, and then toward the one person you have been circling. A request. A call. A neighbor. Do not wait for Easter dinner to make you brave. Dinner is ham. Brave is a walk. I have waited on ham before. Ham does not make a man walk. A date does, if he keeps it. Keep yours. Then tell one man, even if the walk was only ten feet of dirt.

WES: Holy Week is long enough for one walk. It is not long enough for a new delay. Friday is Good Friday. We will tell you what we did with the trees and the dirt. Until then, go first. The last Adam already did. I keep wanting a bigger garden so I can postpone the small one. There is no bigger garden than the one in front of you. A phone. A barn. A car seat. A four o'clock. Walk. Then tell one man you walked, even if you walked badly. Badly is still a walk. A speech about walking is still a tree. I want fewer speeches this week and more dirt on my own knees, even if nobody is keeping score and even if I still want the trees more than I want to admit out loud.
