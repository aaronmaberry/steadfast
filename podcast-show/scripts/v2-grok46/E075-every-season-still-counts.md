## SEGMENT 3 · Hood and pot roast

## SEGMENT 4 · The Word

## SEGMENT 5 · Challenge

WES: This week's challenge. Eat with someone twenty years older or younger. Learn one name you have walked past. Retire one phrase that stores your life in a later season.

JONATHON: If you're 22, your season counts without topping out. If you're 62, your season counts without a farewell tour. If you're divorced, you can still sit at a table that is not a waiting room.

WES: Look around your house this week. The youngest has two years. I can learn a bead. I can learn a name. I can stop being a good ghost.

JONATHON: I'll host again in two weeks. I'll walk the wagon. I'll ask the 61-year-old the hard question and I will not defend myself in the first minute.
