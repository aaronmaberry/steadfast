## SEGMENT 1 · The stock tank

WES: Let's take the next few minutes on Thursday evening. You left at three. He was at the gate.

JONATHON: About six-thirty. Three poles. A coffee can of worms. He said evening. He baited one slowly, handed Ruby a worm, and she asked if the worm can feel it. He said not as much as the fish. She baited her own on the first try. He showed one sidearm cast and never touched her pole again. About twenty minutes in, a bluegill the size of a phone. She held it up to me. I caught nothing. I baited my own hook. I said okay. That was the bravest okay I have said in a while.

WES: He called me after. Second call in a week. He said she is all right. I said Ruby. He said both of them. That is a blessing from a man who does not give speeches. Take it. Do not improve it.

JONATHON: On the drive home she asked what I think about when I am by myself. I said Pancake. Then she said, and if Grandpa in Louisiana liked my picture. She has carried that since winter. I said I do not know, baby. I wonder that too. She said okay. I did not fix it. I wanted to tell her a story that made him kinder than I can prove. I let the not-knowing sit in the truck.

[BOARD: The floor teaches first.]


## SEGMENT 2 · A couch and a ponytail

WES: We're about halfway. Wednesday my daughter told me they bought a couch that did not fit their door. While the son-in-law went for straps she said, I told him. About the waiting. He said he did not know I was waiting. I started to finish her sentence. I do that at home. I give the Thursday table patience and I steal sentences from my own kid. I shut my mouth. They took the feet off the couch. That is the whole story. I did not get to be wise. I got to be quiet.

JONATHON: Friday morning, the morning after yes, tacos, Austin texted first. A first. Question. How do you do a ponytail. Asking for real. His daughter is ten. Summer league, Saturday mornings. Dani filmed a forty-second tutorial on Ruby. Brush it back, hold it with one hand, the hair tie goes around twice, not three times, split and pull tight. Ruby said ow, it is fine. Saturday at eight-ten a photo of a crooked ponytail from behind. Did it. Its crooked. She wore it. A minute later, she said its fine. No moral. I am not adding one.

WES: Good. Do not add one. A man who asked for a ponytail is a father. A girl who wore a crooked one is a daughter. That is enough. I have ruined enough reports by putting a bow on them.

JONATHON: At thirteen I was quiet, door shut, headphones, fine through the door to my mom. A coach drove me home and said you hungry and talked about the Astros. About every third time I said something real. Eyes forward. That is why a drive-through works. A girl will talk to a windshield who will not talk to a face.

[BOARD: Twice around, not three.]


## SEGMENT 3 · The Word

WES: Proverbs twenty-two, verse six. A start, not a guarantee, and still a start.

JONATHON: "Start children off on the way they should go, and even when they are old they will not turn from it."

WES: Start. Off. The way. You cannot start a girl on a way you only use on the phone. You start her on the way you use in a truck when the answer is I do not know. Old is a long time. Start is this week.

JONATHON: I do not want the proverb as a promise I can cash if I do one fish. I want it as a warning. She is already on a way. The way she saw me talk to a contractor. The way I did not lie about Louisiana. Start means I can still change the road under her feet.

[BOARD: Deuteronomy 6:7 (NIV) · "Impress them on your children. Talk about them when you sit at home and when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up."]


## SEGMENT 4 · The drive-through date

WES: Last few minutes. Tool is the drive-through date. Eyes forward. She orders. You do not comment on the order. You ask one thing you do not know. Then you let the music sit there if she wants music. My youngest, sixteen, drives us both to the one by the high school on Thursday nights after supper. He orders the same thing every time. Last month, four sentences about a girl in chemistry, then the music up. I did not chase the four sentences. Chasing is how you get none next week.

JONATHON: If she is a teenager and will not talk, do the drive-through anyway. Do not announce it as a talk. Announce fries. If she says fine, you still bought fries. Fine is not a failure. Fine is a door that is not open yet. Stay in the car. Do not turn it into a speech about how you never had this.

WES: If she is seven, the drive-through still works. If she is grown, a porch works, and you still do not finish her sentence. I almost finished mine. I am glad I did not. I would have stolen a thing that belonged to her and her husband.

JONATHON: I will not send Grandpa the fish photo. Not yet. If he does not answer, then it is her picture he did not answer. I will not make a seven-year-old carry that. I can carry the not-knowing. That is my job. Hers is to bait a hook and ask about a worm.

WES: Look around your house this week. Pick a night. Pick a window. Ask one thing you do not know. Do not grade the answer. Do not post the fish. Let her hold it toward whoever she chooses.

JONATHON: She held it toward me. I keep seeing that. A man I barely know taught her, and she still turned to me. I do not have to be the expert to be the dad. I have to be in the truck on the way home when the real question comes.

[BOARD: Eyes stay on the road.]

WES: I keep wanting to call my daughter back and tidy the mad-at-me line. I will not. Quiet was the gift. I can send a text that says I am still thinking about what you asked. That is not tidying. That is staying.

WES: I keep wanting this to resolve in one conversation, and it will not. The assignment is the next honest sentence, said on time, without a speech attached. I have left too many good talks on a table because I wanted a better closing line than the one I had.

WES: I want this back in your house, not in ours. Pick the one door that is yours. Not your brother's. Not a man on a screen. Yours. Put a day on it before supper, even if the day is tomorrow and the sentence is clumsy.

WES: If you fail the first try, the second try still counts. I have to say that every time or I will use the miss as a way out and then tell a story about the miss like the story was the obedience.

## SEGMENT · Sitting with it

WES: I have watched men collect stories like this and never change a Tuesday. Do not be that man this week. Change one Tuesday. Then tell one person you did, because a secret obedience turns back into a mood by Thursday.

WES: I want the listener who thinks this does not apply because his house is quieter. Quiet is not settled. You already know that if you have sat through a polite holiday and called it peace.

JONATHON: My house can be loud and still be a cease-fire. Volume is not the test. The test is whether the true thing got said to the person who had to hear it, not to a mug at this table.

WES: I will check this on Friday, which is the only reason I trust myself. A public calendar is a kind of mercy for a man who likes his recliner and likes to call the recliner wisdom.

JONATHON: I will hold you to it, and I want somebody holding me. That is why I say the time out loud even when it makes me look late. Looking late is better than looking peaceful and being dug in.

WES: A steadfast man does the small thing while it is still small. That is the whole show, said again, because we forget it by Wednesday and start waiting to feel ready, and the ready never comes first.

JONATHON: I forget it by Tuesday afternoon. Ruby does not. She has a better memory for what I said I would do than any review I have ever run, and she does not care that I had a good reason.

WES: If this feels like too many stories, pick one. One house. One name. One time. That is enough weight for seven days, and it is more than most of us will actually carry if we get proud of the list.

JONATHON: I will pick one. If I pick three I will do none, and I already know that about myself from sales and from home. One is how a man like me gets free of his own notes app.

WES: I have a habit of saving the true sentence for a better room. There is no better room. The kitchen is the room. The truck is the room. The porch after the dishes is the room. Say it there.

JONATHON: I have rehearsed in better rooms my whole adult life. The rehearsal is how I stay safe. Dani can hear the difference between a rehearsal and a sentence. She stops me when I start handing people a door before they are in the room.

WES: Some of you will try to make this spiritual so you do not have to be specific. Prayer is good. A day and a name is still required. Do not baptize delay and call it discernment.

WES: The man who says he will start after the busy season is lying to himself the same way I did in my forties. There is always another season. Do the small thing inside the busy, or you will never do it.

JONATHON: HVAC sales does not hand you a quiet month. If I wait for one I will still be waiting when Theo has a license. I have to put the true thing on a Tuesday that already has a meeting.

WES: I do not need you to feel ready. I need you to be on time. Ready is a feeling. On time is a clock. I have trusted the feeling too long, and it has made me late to people I love.

JONATHON: I wait for ready like it is a weather window. Then the window closes and I tell a funny story about almost doing it. I do not want another funny story. I want a clumsy sentence that landed.
