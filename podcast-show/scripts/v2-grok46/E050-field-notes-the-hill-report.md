## SEGMENT 1 · Not a straight line

WES: Let's take the next few minutes on the shoes, because I do not want anyone thinking Friday is a victory lap. You timed the cry. Then you took over.

JONATHON: Minute six. I had a nine o'clock call. I did the shoes. I said bud twice. I heard myself. Tuesday and Wednesday he did them himself. Thursday he asked for help with one. I said no. He did it. It is not a straight line. It is Theo. I named my hill to Dani and I said I do not know yet. I heard myself say my kids like they are me with extra steps. I heard myself say the widows' crew like I had already gone. I have not gone yet. She listened, then asked what time Saturday, because she needs the car.

WES: I said my hill to my wife on the porch. The family. Keeping them close. Finishing. She said that is your card. Then she went back to her book. I wanted more. She was reading. A heading does not owe you a parade. I am trying to let a book be a book.

JONATHON: Good. I wanted a parade for shoes. I got a car question. That is a woman who believes I might actually go. I want to deserve the car question.

[BOARD: The floor teaches first.]


## SEGMENT 2 · The chain and the games

WES: We're about halfway. Tuesday the youngest said the chain went dull at the end. Uncle said it is dull. Wednesday night the saw was in the shop vise, his phone on a coffee can, a man in Oregon, watched twice, every tooth filed. I stood in the door about a minute. I said night. He said night. That is his now. I did not take the file. I wanted to. My hands know that file. His hands needed it more.

JONATHON: After my dad left I played a baseball video game at a friend's house three or four hours a day for about two years. It was the one place where if I worked at something, it got better. The game taught me how to practice. I just did not have anything real to practice on. I am not romantic about it. I am telling you why a screen can be a hill when a house is not.

WES: A man at the table, forty-one, son fourteen, uses timers, a cord, a router password. That is a fence. It is not a hill. A fence can help. A hill is a Saturday that gets better when you work at it. I do not want us to confuse a password with a creek.

JONATHON: Your oldest gamed all through high school. Friday nights. A headset. You were out of the fog by then. You said turn that off and never sat down. You are going to ask him Sunday what those Friday nights were. I want you to ask before you judge my closet. I still have a closet full of that hunger. I do not want you to unplug a life you have never sat beside.

[BOARD: The chain is his now.]


## SEGMENT 3 · The Word

WES: First Corinthians sixteen, verse thirteen. Four short orders. None of them say unplug a boy you will not sit with.

JONATHON: "Be on your guard; stand firm in the faith; be courageous; be strong."

WES: Guard. Stand. Courage. Strength. That can be a file on a tooth. That can be shoes after a cry. That can be a headset you finally ask about. Courage is not a confiscation. Courage is sitting down.

JONATHON: I can be strong about other people's sons and soft about my own floor. I want the verse in my hallway at seven-thirty, not in a speech about screens. The hallway is where I said bud twice. That is the field.

## SEGMENT 4 · The Saturday project

WES: Last few minutes. Tool is the Saturday project. One real thing that gets better if he works at it. Cedar. A ramp. A chain. Shoes if he is four. Put a date on it. I would unplug a console for a summer if I were scared enough. He said that is easy to say with a brother who has a whole slope of cedar. I said that is not fair. He said no. It is also where he lives. We are unresolved on the closet. We are agreed on Saturday. I can live with unresolved if Saturday is dated.

JONATHON: I am going Saturday. Ruby. Theo. A coffee can of screws. If I skip because a call appears, I will tell you I skipped. I will not tell you a story about almost. Almost is my favorite lie.

WES: Sunday I ask the oldest what those Friday nights were. I will not open with turn that off. I will open with I never sat down. That is my confession, not his. If he tells me it was four guys since sixth grade, I will listen. I have a feeling I am about to find out I missed a brotherhood I called a problem.

JONATHON: If your son only wants games, give him a Saturday that is real, and sit beside the game once so you know what you are talking about. I learned practice from a screen. I needed a hill in dirt. He may need both for a season. Do not lie about that to look holy.

WES: Look around your house this week. Date the project. Stay on the floor when he cries. Do not take the file. Do not take the Velcro unless the call is truly life or death. A nine o'clock call is not death. I have used calls as death for years.

JONATHON: I used the call as death on Monday. I am not proud of it. I am going back to the floor. If I say bud, Theo will catch it. I want to be catchable. That is a hill for me.

[BOARD: Sit down before you unplug.]

WES: I stood in that shop door like a man at a museum. I wanted to be the exhibit. He was the work. Night was the right word. I am practicing night. It is harder than a speech about work ethic. I dated a Saturday for him. I have not dated one for the back door. June heat made the jamb worse. He can file a chain. I can still not plane a door. That is a hill I keep walking around.

JONATHON: Your boy has a vise and a file. You have a plane and a story. I'll ask which one actually got used.

WES: I keep wanting this to resolve in one conversation, and it will not. The assignment is the next honest sentence, said on time, without a speech attached. I have left too many good talks on a table because I wanted a better closing line than the one I had.

JONATHON: Honestly, I will try to add the speech anyway. Somebody should stop me. If you are sitting in a driveway doing that too, write the three sentences down before you dial, then read only those three. The fourth sentence is where I get cute and ruin it.

WES: I want this back in your house, not in ours. Pick the one door that is yours. Not your brother's. Not a man on a screen. Yours. Put a day on it before supper, even if the day is tomorrow and the sentence is clumsy.

JONATHON: Mine is already on a calendar. That is the only reason I trust it. A mood is not a plan, and I have used moods for years to sound wise while I stayed still. I do not want to sound wise. I want to be on time.

## SEGMENT · Sitting with it

WES: If you fail the first try, the second try still counts. I have to say that every time or I will use the miss as a way out and then tell a story about the miss like the story was the obedience.

JONATHON: I used the first miss as a way out for most of my twenties. I am trying not to do that at thirty-seven. It is harder than any capacitor, and it is also more ordinary than I want to admit at this table.

WES: I have watched men collect stories like this and never change a Tuesday. Do not be that man this week. Change one Tuesday. Then tell one person you did, because a secret obedience turns back into a mood by Thursday.

JONATHON: I am the man who collects stories. I have a notes app full of them. The only ones that count are the ones that got a day and a time and a person who knows I said I would do it.

WES: I want the listener who thinks this does not apply because his house is quieter. Quiet is not settled. You already know that if you have sat through a polite holiday and called it peace.

JONATHON: My house can be loud and still be a cease-fire. Volume is not the test. The test is whether the true thing got said to the person who had to hear it, not to a mug at this table.

WES: I will check this on Friday, which is the only reason I trust myself. A public calendar is a kind of mercy for a man who likes his recliner and likes to call the recliner wisdom.

WES: A steadfast man does the small thing while it is still small. That is the whole show, said again, because we forget it by Wednesday and start waiting to feel ready, and the ready never comes first.

JONATHON: I forget it by Tuesday afternoon. Ruby does not. She has a better memory for what I said I would do than any review I have ever run, and she does not care that I had a good reason.

WES: If this feels like too many stories, pick one. One house. One name. One time. That is enough weight for seven days, and it is more than most of us will actually carry if we get proud of the list.

JONATHON: I will pick one. If I pick three I will do none, and I already know that about myself from sales and from home. One is how a man like me gets free of his own notes app.

WES: I have a habit of saving the true sentence for a better room. There is no better room. The kitchen is the room. The truck is the room. The porch after the dishes is the room. Say it there.

JONATHON: I have rehearsed in better rooms my whole adult life. The rehearsal is how I stay safe. Dani can hear the difference between a rehearsal and a sentence. She stops me when I start handing people a door before they are in the room.

WES: Some of you will try to make this spiritual so you do not have to be specific. Prayer is good. A day and a name is still required. Do not baptize delay and call it discernment.

JONATHON: I have baptized delay. I have called it being careful. Careful is sometimes fear with a pressed shirt. I am trying to tell those two apart before I waste another month.
