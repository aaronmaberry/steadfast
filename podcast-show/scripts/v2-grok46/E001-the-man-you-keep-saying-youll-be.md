**Arc.** *Hook:* Wes ate supper in a recliner and called it a regular Tuesday; Jonathon took the long way home so he would miss bath time. *Launch:* who they are, how they met, what Walk Steadfast is, who it is for, and what Tuesday and Friday are. *Tension:* the man you keep describing never arrives, because Tuesday is where he is made. *Turn:* the daughter on the floor by the recliner. *Resolution:* one meal with no phone, one text, and Friday they report.
