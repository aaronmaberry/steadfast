## SEGMENT 2 · Midnight and goggles

WES: Tell the compressor. I want the product on the table, because you have never said out loud what the work keeps alive.

JONATHON: Last August. A west-side branch library that is a city cooling center lost its rooftop unit at six during a heat advisory. A hundred and four outside. A rep, twenty-eight, about three years in, drove the compressor part over at eleven so the tech could install it at six in the morning, before opening. He texted a picture of the part on the loading dock. My first words were, did you bill it as an emergency order. He said yeah, boss. I said good man. He got the same Christmas gift card everybody got. I will call him this week and tell him what I kept. It keeps people alive. The product. I have never said that on this table. I have said numbers. I have not said a library full of people who cannot go home to heat.

WES: That is work before the fall, leaking through a curse. You asked about margin first. You still sent a man at eleven. Both things are true. Tell him the second thing. Do not skip it because the first thing shames you.

JONATHON: The Fourth. Theo wore his swim goggles all night. He said fireworks get in your eyes. Like a tiny welder. Ruby narrated every one like a sportscaster. And here comes a blue one. Keep includes a week in August with the phone in a drawer.

JONATHON: I have been good at work it. I have been bad at take care of it. People. Days. A rep who drove at eleven. Myself. Nine unused days is a man who does not keep the garden. He strips it and calls that faithfulness.

## SEGMENT 4 · The man in the truck

WES: A coworker. About fifteen years at my work. He has eaten lunch alone in his running truck since about April. He used to talk about his boys' baseball. I have walked past for three months. Plan: knock on the window and ask to eat in the cab. If no, ask again Wednesday. Then tell my wife at supper about the man in the truck, and about myself. I have not told her I like the mid-morning problem. I have not told the youngest I like my job. My dad never said. I can say.

JONATHON: Do not make the knock a project with a moral. Take a sandwich. Ask for air conditioning. If he says you need something, tell the truth. You have a sandwich and it is a hundred degrees. That is a garden sentence. Not a rescue speech.

WES: I will not rescue him. I will knock. I may get two inches of window. Two inches is a start. I have used three months of walking past as wisdom. It was fear. I do not know his name well enough to say it here, and that is already a tell.

JONATHON: I will call the midnight rep and say what I kept. I will not say it in a way that makes him my illustration. I will say the library. I will say I asked about the bill first. I will say I am sorry I stopped at good man. Sorry is part of keep.

You do not have to call it a calling to treat it as a garden for the hours you are there. And if you love it, nine unused days is not seriousness. It is a man stripping the garden and calling the dust faithfulness. I have been that man. The VP will clap. Your kids will not remember the clap.

WES: I left a farm because I thought work there was a curse. Some of it was sweat after the fall. Some of it was a pecan and a trough and a brother placed. I was too young to sort those. I can sort them now. Liking a part is not betraying the part I left.

JONATHON: A column that grades rest can become a new idol. I will not let South Central leading on rest become my plaque. I will go to the beach, or the house, or the pancake, and I will be a man who is not available. Unavailable is a kind of keep.


## SEGMENT 6 · This week's challenge

WES: Tell one person what you like about the work, even a sliver. Knock on one window you have walked past. If you have been hoarding days, put rest on a calendar. Do not wait for a slogan to approve it.

WES: Last few minutes. I will knock. I will tell my wife at supper. I will tell the youngest I like my job, and I will let him say huh and ask for potatoes. Huh is a holy word in my house this summer.

JONATHON: I will take August. I will call the rep. I will not decorate the column. If I come back proud of a bar chart, stop me. The bar chart is not the garden.

WES: I keep seeing that truck in the lot with the engine on. I have been a man in a truck. I have also been a man who walks past. I know both sins. The sandwich is how I stop being the second one without pretending I can fix the first.

WES: I keep wanting this to resolve in one conversation, and it will not. The assignment is the next honest sentence, said on time, without a speech attached. I have left too many good talks on a table because I wanted a better closing line than the one I had.

WES: I want this back in your house, not in ours. Pick the one door that is yours. Not your brother's. Not a man on a screen. Yours. Put a day on it before supper, even if the day is tomorrow and the sentence is clumsy.
