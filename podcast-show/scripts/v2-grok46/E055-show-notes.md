**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
What do you do when you don't feel it anymore? And why do we treat the feeling like the fire?

Jonathon's seven-year-old came home from Vacation Bible School and asked if he's on fire for Jesus. He said, "Some days." She said, "Which days?" He told the flat year after his first year of faith. Wes told the decade when everybody said he was on fire and his wife asked, "Where are we going?" They open 2 Corinthians 5:15: there is no feeling in that verse. The fire is what He did. Plus Virgil, seventy-seven, who has fixed widows' porches every Saturday since 1998. Does he like it? "Some Saturdays."

This week's challenge: name one thing God has already put in front of you that you've been waiting to feel like doing. Do it this week anyway.

## This week's challenge

1. **Name** one thing God has already put in front of you that you've been waiting to feel like doing.
2. **Do it this week** without the feeling. Notice afterward whether the feeling showed up.
3. **Married:** ask your wife, "Is there something you've seen me waiting to feel like doing?" Say your guess first.
4. **Single:** find the next thing. A crew. A younger back. A Tuesday.
5. If the flat has turned dark, tell someone. If it scares you, call or text 988.

Then tell us how it went at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
