**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
What does a godly man actually look like? One sentence, four verbs, and what each one looks like on a regular Tuesday. Wes Hart and Jonathon fill in the back of the card.

Wes says the sentence once, then they take it apart: a gutter that is finally done, a dad who dropped the weight, the scariest text Jonathon ever sent, and what "God's pay" means (an inheritance, not a wage you earn). They disagree about which mark is hardest. Philippians 2:5. Jonathon pushes back. Lists don't change anybody, and "go first" gets misused by bossy men.

This week's challenge: write the sentence on the back of your card. Pick the mark you're weakest in and tell one man.

## This week's challenge

1. Write the sentence on the **back of your card**.
2. Pick the **one mark** you're weakest in.
3. **Tell one man** which one.
4. **Married:** ask your wife which one she sees you do best, and which she'd love to see more.
5. **Single:** ask a friend the same two questions.
6. Every night, the **one-out-of-four** check before bed.
