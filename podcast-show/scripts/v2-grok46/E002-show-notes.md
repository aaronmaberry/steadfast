Tuesday's challenge was small: one meal with no phone, one question at the table, one text to one man. Friday is the receipt.

Wes asked his wife what Tuesday is like for her. She has been driving his mother to physical therapy every week since August. His text to his oldest son got a report card: "Are you dying?" Jonathon failed the first night when Ruby said, "Daddy, you said." Thursday he tried again. Dani told him about the pretending. He offered to buy blackout curtains they already owned.

Luke 16:10 again: the little thing is still the test. A question men ask a lot: how do you start if you've never been the spiritual one? Pray one sentence. Tell her. Read Mark. Tool on the table: the phone basket, yours first.
