**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
What kind of man are you on a regular Tuesday?

This is the launch of Walk Steadfast. Wes Hart and Jonathon Hale introduce each other, tell how they met (a men's breakfast, eleven minutes in a parking lot, and the scariest text Jonathon ever sent a man), and lay out what the show is: two men at a table, a main conversation every Tuesday, and Field Notes every Friday, where they report on the challenge.

Then they get into the question. Jonathon's long way home on Loop 1604. Wes's fog years and the night his daughter brought her plate to the recliner. 1 Corinthians 13:11 and Luke 16:10. Rest versus hiding. And a first challenge small enough to actually do.

This week's challenge: one meal with no phone, one question at the table, and a text to one man. Friday they report.

## This week's challenge

1. **One meal with no phone.** In another room or in the truck. Face-down does not count.
2. **Ask one question.** Married: "What's Tuesday like for you?" Then listen. Don't fix. Single: eat with somebody and ask about his regular week. Alone: invite a man, or eat at the table and pray out loud.
3. **Text one man.** "Hey, how's your Tuesday?"

Then tell us how it went at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
