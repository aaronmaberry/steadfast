## SEGMENT 2 · A sister and a badge

JONATHON: The twenty-four-year-old at car-care, no car, overnights at a warehouse, early bus home, sleeps till two. Raised by his grandmother, now in the nursing home by the hospital. ## SEGMENT 4 · The one-page house plan

WES: Last few minutes. Tool is a one-page house plan. Three lines. What you will do at home. Who you will keep. What you will refuse to manage that's not yours. Say it on a porch. Don't laminate it unless your wife already owns a laminator and you're making a joke.

JONATHON: What stuck this season. Austin, four Thursdays of did you eat. K, K, K, then yes. tacos. That's a heading I didn't write that still happened because I kept a Thursday. What isn't stuck is a second question to Dani's dad. I'm scared the second answer won't be as good. That's a bad reason and it's mine.

WES: What stuck for me is two calls with my little brother since Deputy, plus Abilene, plus a Friday-noon call about a cold. What isn't stuck is the barn. Two Saturdays, one missed, and four sentences if you count the phone. Hand me that. Same time next week. Cows will be here. He told me that too. Four sentences is a start. It's not a heading yet. The card is the heading. The barn is how I heed it.

JONATHON: If you can't write three lines, steal the shape, not the words. Home. A person. A refuse. Then tell one person in the house. If you only tell a table of men, you have a slogan again.

WES: Look around your house this week. The fridge is allowed to hold the plan. The porch is allowed to hear it. The week will try to pull your eyes back to the row. Look at the tree anyway.

JONATHON: I won't plan Tuesday nights. I will do dishes. I will call my mom and ask about her first. If I come back with a story about a badge I improved for a young man, stop me. That's me wandering with a toolbox again.

## SEGMENT · Sitting with it

WES: I've watched men collect headings like this and never change a jamb. Don't be that man this week. Write three lines. Say them on a porch. Then tell one person you did, because a secret plan turns back into a mood by Thursday.

JONATHON: I'm the man who collects stories. I've got a notes app and a fridge with Ruby's heading on it. The only ones that count are the ones a child can read on the way to cereal.

WES: I want the listener who thinks this doesn't apply because his house is quieter. Quiet isn't settled. You already know that if you've sat through a polite holiday and called it peace. A heading can be a phone that finally rings the other way.

JONATHON: My house can be loud and still be a wander. Volume isn't the test. The test is whether I call my mom and ask about her first, not whether I can tell you about a Sharpie badge.

WES: I'll check the door on Friday, which is the only reason I trust myself. The plane has to move, or the line is a slogan. A public calendar is a kind of mercy for a man who likes his recliner and likes to call the recliner wisdom.

JONATHON: I'll hold you to the jamb, and I want somebody holding me to Tuesday nights. That's why I say the three lines out loud even when it makes me look late. Looking late is better than looking visionary and being dug in.

WES: A steadfast man does the small thing while it's still small. For me that's a swollen back door I keep walking around. For you that's dishes and a mom call. We forget it by Wednesday and start waiting to feel ready.

JONATHON: I forget it by Tuesday afternoon. Ruby doesn't. Be nice to Theo most of the time is already on the fridge. She has a better memory for what I said I'd do than any review I've ever run.

WES: If this feels like too many stories, pick one. One fridge. One porch. One jamb. That's enough weight for seven days.

JONATHON: I'll pick Tuesday nights. If I pick three I'll do none. One refuse is how a man like me gets free of his own notes app.

WES: I've got a habit of saving the true sentence for a better room. There is no better room. The porch is the room. The fridge is the room. The door that still sticks is the room. Say it there.

JONATHON: I've rehearsed in better rooms my whole adult life. The rehearsal is how I stay safe. Dani can hear the difference between a heading and a ticket. She stops me when I start handing people a badge before they're in the room.

WES: Some of you will try to make this spiritual so you don't have to be specific. Prayer is good. Three lines is still required. Don't baptize delay and call it discernment. Habakkuk said write it so a herald can carry it.

JONATHON: I've baptized delay. I've called it being careful. Careful is sometimes fear with a pressed shirt. Most of the time is an honest word for a seven-year-old and for me.

WES: The man who says he'll start after the busy season is lying to himself the same way I did lifting and shoving a door I wouldn't plane. There is always another season. Write the lines inside the busy, or you never will.

JONATHON: HVAC sales doesn't hand you a quiet month. If I wait for one I'll still be waiting when Theo has a license. I've got to put the heading on a Tuesday that already has a meeting, and I've got to leave her Tuesday nights alone.
