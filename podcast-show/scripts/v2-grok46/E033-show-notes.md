How does a man leave his father and mother without slamming the door?

Wes waved his son to the mailbox and finally understood his mother's screen door. Jonathon drove to Pasadena with nothing to fix. His mom said she does not know what she is for. Genesis 2:24 says leave, then unite. Not slam, then ghost. Also in this episode: an anniversary battery, a counselor's question, a Thursday-table man who told the truth on a porch, and watercolor on Saturday mornings.
