**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
What do you do when home never handed you off?

Wes left at eighteen and his mother told him he was a man now. Jonathon is still the man his mom FaceTimes when the AC truck pulls up. He has sat at every dealership desk since he was sixteen. He said "Not bad" to a thirty-one-year-old and heard the old manager in his mouth.

Luke 2:49: Jesus names the Father's house, then goes home and honors the people who raised him. Leaving and honoring in the same breath. This week's challenge: hand one job back. Come eat. Let her talk to the man.
