What if there is no wife yet? And why do we treat a single man like he's in the lobby of his own life?

Merle found a lead singer without Wes. The 24-year-old who brings donuts to car-care sang "Blessed Assurance" from the back row. His text: nights, rent, and his grandmother's nails. Jonathon confesses the "Anybody special?" text. Wes tells the Tuesday he shaved his granddad and heard, "You did all right, Sport." 1 Corinthians 7:17: live as a believer in the situation the Lord has assigned. Married men, quit the lobby questions.
