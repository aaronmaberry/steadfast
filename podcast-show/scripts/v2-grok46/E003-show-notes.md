**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
What is a man? Most of us can't answer in one sentence, and Jonathon found out in the bathtub when his four-year-old asked.

Wes Hart and Jonathon on why you're not crazy for wanting a definition, and why you already have one, whether you've read it or not. Wes tells the story of a diner receipt and the bad sentence he lived for fifteen years. Jonathon writes his on Wes's feed-store receipt. Then Genesis 1:27, what it means for a man to be made on purpose, and why that's not a ranking.

This week's challenge: write down the sentence you've actually been living, even if it's a bad one. Read it to one man. Married: ask your wife what she thinks your sentence is.

## This week's challenge

1. Write down the sentence you've actually been living. Not the church answer. The real one.
2. **Tell one man.** Read it to him.
3. **Married:** on a calm night, ask your wife what she thinks your sentence is. Say yours first. If it stings, sit with it a day.
4. **Single:** ask a friend who's seen you at your worst, "When things go sideways, what do I do?" Then offer him his.

Then tell us how it went at walksteadfast.com, or tag @steadfastwalk with #WalkSteadfast.
