## SEGMENT 1 · Check the box

WES: Let's take the next few minutes on seven o'clock, because I promised a sentence. He picked up on the first ring. "I've been waiting on you since the barn." I said, "Did you ask her?" He said, "Did you?" Neither of us had. We are asking her together at the April pot roast, Sunday the eleventh. I will say nothing until she is done. The call was an hour and four minutes. Longest of our adult lives. I did not hide in the ham. I sat in the truck. I told my wife the number when I came in. She said she was wrong, and then she told me not to say so here, and then she said I could, Thursday, since she was only wrong once.

## SEGMENT 2 · From the table

WES: We're about halfway, and I want the question. Does it matter if I don't feel it?

JONATHON: Thursday I did not feel well pleased. I felt like a man brushing his teeth with a verse. I wanted a glow. I got paste. If feeling is the test I failed. If the words are the test I still said them, three mornings out of four, and I told the truth about the fourth.

WES: It matters that you say it. It does not matter that you glow. Glow is a cousin of the leaderboard. Paste is closer to a vow. I have had years of glow that did not change a Tuesday. I have had toothbrush mornings that did. If you wait to feel like a beloved son you will wait through another Easter. Say the words. Then go ask your mother what she wants. Then stay ten minutes after a sentence with no because. Feeling can catch up. Or not. The walk still counts.

JONATHON: My nephew asked by text if God was disappointed he did not feel different on day eleven. I wrote, "No. Keep Thursday. Keep the roommate. Feeling is slow." He wrote, "ok. slow." I needed slow too. I checked the app more than I will say. No message. A like is still the last thing. I wanted a paragraph. I got silence. I said my sentences anyway. That's the only half I own. I told Dani I had checked again. She said, "Stop making him the weather." She is right. He is a man in Louisiana with a thumb. He is not the climate of my kitchen. If I let him be the climate I will skip Ruby again and call it grief. It would be a hide with a holy name.

WES: Stop making him the weather is a sentence I needed twenty years ago. I made my dad's silence the climate and then I made a title the climate and then I made a fog the climate. Climate is how a man avoids a Tuesday. Tuesday is a call, a card, a walk. I want Tuesdays back. I have enough climate. I told my wife that after the call. She said, "Then keep asking me what I want too." I had not asked her about the downstairs-room idea either. I had a whole plan for my mother and I had not asked the woman I live with. That's the shape again. I wrote it down. I will not run two kitchens at once.
