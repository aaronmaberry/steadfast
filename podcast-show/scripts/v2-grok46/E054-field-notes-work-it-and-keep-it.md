## SEGMENT 1 · Door's open

WES: Let's take the next few minutes on a cab I had walked past. He looked at me a long time. Then, door's open. Twenty-five minutes about the Rangers. They are bad this year. I said same time tomorrow. He shrugged. Wednesday the door was unlocked before I got there. Thursday he said you do not have to do this. Short. Not friendly. I said I know. It is air-conditioned. No laugh. Back to the bullpen. I do not know what it is. I will not ask yet. Asking too early is how I turn a sandwich into a project.

JONATHON: Tuesday at supper your wife asked the man's name. You did not have one she knew. She said you have never told me the name of anybody you work with. Mostly true. You did not know if he has kids. That is a man who likes the mid-morning problem and still keeps the people at work as scenery. I have done that with reps. I know their numbers. I do not know their grandmothers.

WES: Wednesday at supper I told the youngest I like my job. He said okay. I said because my dad never told me if he liked his. He said huh. Can I have the rest of the potatoes. That is the review. I will take potatoes. I wanted a moment. I got a second helping. Second helpings are how my people bless a sentence.

JONATHON: Monday I called the midnight rep. I said what I kept. He said I did not do it for the margin, boss. I said I know. I did. His grandmother used to spend summer afternoons at a library like that. Her AC was broken and she would not let anyone fix it. He thought of her the whole drive. Then twenty minutes on a quote after. I let the quote be the rest of the love. I did not tack on a speech about gardens.

[BOARD: Two inches is a door.]


## SEGMENT 2 · A bar chart and a driveway

WES: We're about halfway. The column. Week one.

JONATHON: Two reps put in for time off. The VP emailed the company a bar chart. South Central leading on rest. I hated the chart. I was glad about the two reps. Both things are true. The thirty-one-year-old does not want his vacation days. I will not tell him what to do with them. Ask is not tell. I am practicing ask. I have been a tell man my whole sales life.

WES: My shutdown has been the same for twenty years. I empty my pockets on the bedroom dresser before I go into the kitchen. Keys. Wallet. Knife. Change. The receipt with the names. My wife can tell when I skip it, from my face. The work comes into the kitchen in my shoulders. The dresser is a gate. I watched my brother shut the pasture gate behind the truck every night. He never said it was a shutdown. It was a shutdown.

JONATHON: Mine is supposed to be five-thirty. Phone in the truck console until the kids are in bed. Tuesday I made it. Wednesday I made it. Thursday a customer email at six-ten. Forty minutes in the driveway. Dani sat in the passenger seat without a word, then said supper's cold. The answer was not needed until Monday. I wanted to finish the email so I could be present. I was already absent. Present is a gate I skipped.

[BOARD: He thought of his grandmother.]


## SEGMENT 3 · The Word

WES: Colossians three, verse twenty-three. All your heart, and still not for the masters.

JONATHON: "Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters,"

WES: All your heart is allowed. That is the like. Not for human masters is the keep. A bar chart is a human master. A customer at six-ten is a human master. A plaque wall is a human master. You can work hard and still shut the gate. If you cannot shut the gate, the heart already has a different lord.

JONATHON: I like the work a lot. That is not wrong. Skipping the console is how like becomes a yoke. I asked if it was wrong to like it. It is wrong to like it so much I make my wife eat cold food while I serve a master who will email again at six-forty. The verse gave me both halves. I had been using the first half as cover.

[BOARD: Genesis 2:15 (NIV) · "The Lord God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden to work it and take care of it."]


## SEGMENT 4 · The end-of-day shutdown

WES: Last few minutes. Tool is the end-of-day shutdown. Pockets on a dresser. Phone in a console. A pasture gate. Pick one physical place the work has to stop. If you skip it, your face will tell on you. Let somebody who loves you have permission to see the face. Do not make them sit in a driveway to get a vote.

JONATHON: I will put the phone in the console Friday and Monday, and I will not use Thursday's miss as a way out. If I fail again I will get out of the truck when she sits down. The email can wait. A cold supper is a board I did not ask for and I still have to read.

WES: Is it wrong to like your job a lot. No. It is wrong to like it past the gate. It is wrong to walk past a truck for three months and call that professionalism. It is wrong to never tell a boy you like the work, and then wonder why he only hears you complain. I told him. He asked for potatoes. That is a good keep.

JONATHON: I will not tell the thirty-one-year-old what to do with his days. I will ask if he booked the three. If he says no, I will not make him my pilot. I have a week in August. That is my garden. His is his. Ask is not a bar chart.

WES: Look around your house this week. Empty the pockets. Shut the gate. Knock again if the window only came down two inches. Do not demand a story. Eat. Talk about a bad bullpen. Go inside.

JONATHON: I will call my face what it is when I skip the console. Absent. Not busy. Absent. If Dani has to say supper's cold again, I hope I am already reaching for the door handle.

[BOARD: Keys on the dresser first.]

WES: I still do not know if he has kids. I can live without knowing a week longer. Knowing can be another kind of taking. A sandwich is enough. Tomorrow I will knock if the lot has the truck. If it does not, I will not invent a reason.

WES: I keep wanting this to resolve in one conversation, and it will not. The assignment is the next honest sentence, said on time, without a speech attached. I have left too many good talks on a table because I wanted a better closing line than the one I had.

WES: I want this back in your house, not in ours. Pick the one door that is yours. Not your brother's. Not a man on a screen. Yours. Put a day on it before supper, even if the day is tomorrow and the sentence is clumsy.

JONATHON: Mine is already on a calendar. That is the only reason I trust it. A mood is not a plan, and I have used moods for years to sound wise while I stayed still. I do not want to sound wise. I want to be on time.

WES: If you fail the first try, the second try still counts. I have to say that every time or I will use the miss as a way out and then tell a story about the miss like the story was the obedience.

## SEGMENT · Sitting with it

WES: I have watched men collect stories like this and never change a Tuesday. Do not be that man this week. Change one Tuesday. Then tell one person you did, because a secret obedience turns back into a mood by Thursday.

JONATHON: I am the man who collects stories. I have a notes app full of them. The only ones that count are the ones that got a day and a time and a person who knows I said I would do it.

WES: I want the listener who thinks this does not apply because his house is quieter. Quiet is not settled. You already know that if you have sat through a polite holiday and called it peace.

JONATHON: My house can be loud and still be a cease-fire. Volume is not the test. The test is whether the true thing got said to the person who had to hear it, not to a mug at this table.

WES: I will check this on Friday, which is the only reason I trust myself. A public calendar is a kind of mercy for a man who likes his recliner and likes to call the recliner wisdom.

JONATHON: I will hold you to it, and I want somebody holding me. That is why I say the time out loud even when it makes me look late. Looking late is better than looking peaceful and being dug in.

WES: A steadfast man does the small thing while it is still small. That is the whole show, said again, because we forget it by Wednesday and start waiting to feel ready, and the ready never comes first.

JONATHON: I forget it by Tuesday afternoon. Ruby does not. She has a better memory for what I said I would do than any review I have ever run, and she does not care that I had a good reason.

WES: If this feels like too many stories, pick one. One house. One name. One time. That is enough weight for seven days, and it is more than most of us will actually carry if we get proud of the list.

JONATHON: I will pick one. If I pick three I will do none, and I already know that about myself from sales and from home. One is how a man like me gets free of his own notes app.

WES: I have a habit of saving the true sentence for a better room. There is no better room. The kitchen is the room. The truck is the room. The porch after the dishes is the room. Say it there.

JONATHON: I have rehearsed in better rooms my whole adult life. The rehearsal is how I stay safe. Dani can hear the difference between a rehearsal and a sentence. She stops me when I start handing people a door before they are in the room.

WES: Some of you will try to make this spiritual so you do not have to be specific. Prayer is good. A day and a name is still required. Do not baptize delay and call it discernment.
