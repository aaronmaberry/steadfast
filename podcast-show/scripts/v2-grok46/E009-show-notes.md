**Episode description (for podcast apps and YouTube)**
How does a man tell the weight with his name on it from the weight that is not his?

Jonathon and Dani have said "we should do a will" for three years. The hard line is who raises the kids. Wes signed yellow stickers in 2002 and did not know who the guardian was until he was fifty. Then the opposite pile: he paid for his sixteen-year-old's tailgate and called it grace. The boy learned the truck weighed nothing.

Galatians 6:5. Every man carries his own duffel. Nobody carries a couch alone. Jonathon pushes back: isn't that just bootstraps? Then he names the holiday happiness he has carried since he was nine.

This week's challenge: one weight that is yours, one that is not. Name both.
