Privacy

Short on purpose. We do not need your whole life to sell you a path.

We collect a name and an email when you ask to begin, join the morning letter, or inquire about a group. That is the inventory.

When Stripe (or another processor) is connected, payment data is handled by that processor. We do not store card numbers on this site.

The pair. One brother for six weeks. The app stores the name he typed and the time of the last ping. It does not store the body of the text, why he missed, his contacts, or his location. The ping is device to device. Steadfast does not keep the message. Either man can drop the pair. Both copies go. The pastor, the table, and the house do not see the pair.

The table. A room may see a count, five of seven did the act. It does not see names, timestamps, or a board. After a mark closes, individual check-ins are not kept as a score.

The pages. Workbook answers stay on his login. They are not a group feed and they are not training data. A printed workbook never leaves his table.

We do not sell lists. We do not run dark patterns to keep a man subscribed. Unsubscribe will be one click once the letter is live.

Analytics, if added later, will be written here before they are switched on. Until then this site does not profile you. No third-party pixel on Daily or Login until it is named on this page.
