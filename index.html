The measure

Four marks.
No fifth.

01

Stop Waiting

When it’s yours to do, start now, before you feel like it.

“If anyone, then, knows the good they ought to do and doesn’t do it, it is sin for them.” James 4:17 NIV

02

Pick Up the Weight

Carry what’s yours, even when somebody else dropped it.

“For each one should carry their own load.” Galatians 6:5 NIV

03

Go First

Step out ahead of your people, scared or not.

“When he has brought out all his own, he goes on ahead of them, and his sheep follow him because they know his voice.” John 10:4 NIV

04

Work for God’s Pay

Do it for the Lord’s reward, not the room’s applause.

“Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters, since you know that you will receive an inheritance from the Lord as a reward. It is the Lord Christ you are serving.” Colossians 3:23-24 NIV
