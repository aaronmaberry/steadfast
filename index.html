The measure

Four marks.
No fifth.

01

Reject passivity

Stop waiting for the room to captain itself.

"If anyone, then, knows the good they ought to do and doesn't do it, it is sin for them." James 4:17 NIV

02

Accept responsibility

Name what is yours. Carry it.

"Fathers, do not exasperate your children; instead, bring them up in the training and instruction of the Lord." Ephesians 6:4 NIV

03

Lead courageously

Go first when it costs you.

"Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go." Joshua 1:9 NIV

04

Expect God's reward

Build for a yes that outlasts the week.

"Anyone who comes to him must believe that he exists and that he rewards those who earnestly seek him." Hebrews 11:6 NIV
