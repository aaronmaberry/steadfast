The path · 24 weeks

A plan for any man, in any season.

Young. Mid-life. Older. Single or the head of a house. The weeks do not change. A guided lecture, pages that keep you honest, an act in the real world, and brothers who will ask if you did it. That is the work. What is inside sits below.

The agenda

Twenty-four weeks. Named.

Lecture, pages, and the week’s act follow this spine. Files land in Login and in the app when Wes locks each week. Leader scripts for churches live on Groups.

Weeks 01–06

Reject passivity

  1. 01 Name the drift in your own house.
  2. 02 Stop waiting for someone else to captain the room.
  3. 03 Put the phone down at the table.
  4. 04 Tell the truth about the quiet sins.
  5. 05 Show up to church as a man, not a guest.
  6. 06 Close the first mark.

Weeks 07–12

Accept responsibility

  1. 07 Write what is yours.
  2. 08 Provide without theater.
  3. 09 Confess what you have left half-done.
  4. 10 Carry the calendar of your house.
  5. 11 Make a plan a boy can see.
  6. 12 Close the second mark.

Weeks 13–18

Lead courageously

  1. 13 Go first in the hard conversation.
  2. 14 Pray out loud where your people can hear it.
  3. 15 Protect what you will not joke about.
  4. 16 Discipline without rage.
  5. 17 Lead when it costs you the easy room.
  6. 18 Close the third mark.

Weeks 19–24

Expect God’s reward

  1. 19 Lift your eyes past payday.
  2. 20 Work as worship, not as a brand.
  3. 21 Stay when leaving would be simpler.
  4. 22 Bless your son with a future, not a mood.
  5. 23 Hold the greater city in view.
  6. 24 Finish. Begin again as a different man.

Before you pay

Straight answers.

Who is this for?

Christian men at every stage — young, mid-life, older — who are done drifting. A husband. A father. A single man who will be one. The weeks do not change with the age.

What does a week look like?

Watch the guided lecture. Work the pages. Do the act. Talk it with other men. If you lead a church table, that kit is on Groups.

Can a church buy this once for the ministry?

Yes — as a separate purchase. Groups is the door for volume seats, lecture scripts, and workbooks for every man in the ministry. This page is the personal path.

Is the book included?

Ebook and audiobook come with the training plan. Hardcover, paperback, and the printed workbook are sold separately in Shop.

When do the videos and workbook land?

You buy the path now. Files and member login open week by week as Wes locks the scripts. The app shows the same weeks the site does.

I already read a manhood book.

Reading is not the work. This is a lecture, a page, and an act you cannot hide behind a finished chapter.
