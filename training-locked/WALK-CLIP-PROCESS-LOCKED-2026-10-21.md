## Generate
Grok Imagine image-to-video (grok.com/imagine or xAI API), Video, 10s, 720p. Alternate direction slot to slot. Rotate pace phrase: steady unhurried / purposeful / slow thoughtful.

## Prompt (swap screen-right/left)
He walks 3-4 real full strides across the empty stage to screen-right, realistic human gait with visible weight shift and alternating legs, knees bending, feet planting on the floor, steady unhurried pace. His arms hang relaxed and swing gently at his sides; hands stay low and loose, no raised gestures, real-looking hands with five normal fingers. Camera pans smoothly to follow him, keeping framing from the knees up. He keeps his head turned toward the audience in the direction he walks and never turns to face the camera. He is the only person visible, empty stage with no props, no microphone. Preserve exact face, hair, beard, black henley with sleeves pushed up, lighting, and STEADFAST MEN backdrop. One continuous shot.

## QC (every clip, before Aaron sees it) - reject on any fail
1. Clearly travels with real steps  2. No other person ever  3. No podium/lectern/Bible/book/table/chair/prop
4. No mic  5. Hands natural, 5 fingers, no blobs  6. Never looks into camera  7. No face morph
No footage reused across the 24 lessons.

## Then
Lock footage -> lay locked audio -> lip-sync (sync.so lipsync-2-pro) -> teeth pass (tools/teeth_tone.py --darken 0.35) -> boards (Geist / Geist Mono) -> fades, no black frames.
