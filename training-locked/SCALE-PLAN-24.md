# 24-session scale plan (waist-up, lip-synced, never-repeat plates)

Measured on this S01 v2 proof (184 s):
- 28 Wes shots in 184 s; boards/splits cover ~25%. Avg Wes shot 6.0 s.
- Plate yield: 51 image gens -> 34 passed wardrobe/framing/gaze (67%). A further 5 of 34 failed in render (eyes/prop) -> ~57% of gens end up on screen.
- Render rework: 5 of 31 Avatar IV clips re-rendered (16%).

Per 30-min session (1,800 s), keeping the same cut rhythm:
- Wes on screen ~1,350 s -> ~225 unique plates/clips.
- Image gens ~225 / 0.57 = ~395 gens x ~$0.50 = ~$200 (ElevenLabs credits ~1.2M).
- HeyGen Avatar IV ~1,350 s x 1.16 rework = ~1,570 s of renders per session.

All 24 sessions:
- ~5,400 unique plates, ~9,500 image gens, ~$4,750 in image credits.
- ~37,700 s (~10.5 h) of HeyGen lip-sync renders. Check your HeyGen plan's per-second rate; at the ~0.32 credit/s rate seen so far, ~12,000 credits.
- Walk shots: image_to_video + create_lipsync, ~2 per session (~48 total).

Cheaper levers (pick any):
1. Longer shots: 9-10 s average instead of 6 s -> ~3,600 plates, about -35%.
2. More board/split time (30-35%) -> another -10 to -15%.
3. Stricter prompt (pure profile/three-quarter only) to lift yield from 57% toward 75%.
4. Pilot first: build S02-S03 the same way and re-measure yield before committing the full budget.
